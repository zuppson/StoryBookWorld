Fall

by Scott Mann

1 Chapter · 2 min read

Synopsis

The survival thriller Fall follows two adventurous best friends, Becky and Hunter, who climb a decommissioned, 2,000-foot TV tower in the middle of the Mojave Desert to scatter the ashes of Becky’s late husband.

Chapter 1: Stranded Above the Desert

A year after witnessing her husband Dan plummet to his death in a rock-climbing accident, a grief-stricken Becky is convinced by her thrill-seeking best friend, Hunter, to scale the abandoned B67 TV tower in the Mojave Desert to scatter Dan's ashes and find closure.

They successfully reach the miniature platform at the very top, but as they begin their descent, the corroded ladder detaches completely, marooning them thousands of feet in the air without water or a communication signal. As days pass, the women face dehydration, predatory vultures, and a devastating emotional fracture when Becky realizes Hunter had a secret affair with Dan before his death.

In a desperate move to retrieve their backpack from a satellite dish lower down the structure, Hunter climbs down but falls; though Becky seemingly pulls her back up, a shocking psychological twist reveals that Hunter actually died in the drop and Becky has been hallucinating her companion due to delirium.

Pushed to her absolute survival limits, Becky kills an attacking vulture for sustenance, climbs down to Hunter's physical corpse, and stuffs her cell phone into Hunter's shoe—dropping the body to the ground so the shoe can shield the phone until it falls into cellular range and successfully transmits a distress text.

This final act of grim ingenuity allows Becky to be rescued and finally reunite with her worried father.
