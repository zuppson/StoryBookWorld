Synopsis

The original Saw (2004) is a psychological horror thriller directed by James Wan.

Chapter 1: Chained in the Dark

The story follows two strangers, a photographer named Adam and an oncologist named Dr. Lawrence Gordon, who wake up chained to pipes in a dilapidated, grimy public bathroom with a corpse lying between them.

Through hidden microcassettes, they discover they are pawns in a deadly game orchestrated by the notorious Jigsaw Killer, a terminally ill man named John Kramer who forces his victims into horrific, lethal traps to test their will to live. To save his family, Dr. Gordon is given a strict deadline to kill Adam, leading to a desperate struggle for survival and a legendary twist ending.
