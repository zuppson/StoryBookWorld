Synopsis

2024 South Korean action-comedy film directed by Heo Myeong-haeng and the fourth installment in The Roundup / Crime City franchise.

Chapter 1: The Online Gambling Ring

Teaming up with the Cyber Investigation Team, Ma discovers that the victim was coerced into working for a massive, multi-million dollar illegal online gambling syndicate.

The ruthless ring is operated from Metro Manila by Baek Chang-ki (Kim Mu-yeol), a lethal, cold-blooded former special forces mercenary, who has partnered with tech-genius CEO Chang Dong-chul (Lee Dong-hwi) to establish a monopoly over the illegal casino market through kidnapping, assault, and murder.

When a financial double-cross forces Baek to return to South Korea to violently settle a score, Detective Ma—alongside a hilariously tricked and conscripted returning gangster, Jang Yi-soo (Park Ji-hwan)—orchestrates a massive trap to dismantle the entire cybercrime syndicate and deliver Ma's signature powerhouse, bone-crushing justice.
