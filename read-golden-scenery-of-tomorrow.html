Synopsis

a 2025–2026 Philippine romantic drama series on Viva One. Two childhood best friends grow up together while navigating love, dreams, and new challenges.

Chapter 1: Best Friends, Secret Hearts

Tells the story of lifelong best friends Avianna "Via" Diaz, a quiet architecture student, and Larkin "Arkin" Sanchez, a film student who suddenly shoots to fame as a major celebrity. As their close friendship deepens into a secret romance, they face severe friction from the public eye and obsessive fans, which triggers Via's intense anxiety and forces them down separate paths.

Seeking peace and space to heal, Via moves to Spain to pursue her architectural career, leading to years of heartbreak, maturity, and painful distance. Ultimately, a new project brings Via back to the Philippines, where she and Arkin face their unresolved feelings, find the courage to confront the past, and finally build the bright, public future together they had always dreamed of.
