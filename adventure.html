Journey to the Center of Earth

by Jules Verne · Adventure

When geology professor Trevor Anderson and his nephew Shawn travel to Iceland with local guide Hannah to retrace the steps of Trevor’s missing brother Max, a sudden lightning storm forces them to take shelter in a cave where a landslide seals their exit. Trapped deep underground, they navigate abandoned mine shafts and glowing cavern networks before falling through a fragile layer of rock into a vast, hidden ecosystem at the center of the Earth. Facing extreme heat, giant prehistoric predators like a Giganotosaurus, and dangerous underground oceans, the trio uses Max's old journal and makeshift resources to cross the subterranean realm and trigger a volcanic eruption that safely blasts them back to the surface.

Jumanji: Welcome to the World

by Chris Van Allsburg · Adventure

When high school students Spencer, Fridge, Bethany, and Martha find an old video game console while cleaning out their school's basement, starting the game accidentally transports them into the wild world of Jumanji as the wildly mismatched adult avatars they selected. To escape and return to the real world, the teens must work together to complete a dangerous quest involving a stolen jewel, while navigating their avatars' specific video game strengths and hilarious weaknesses. Facing aggressive wildlife, a ruthless warlord, and a limited pool of shared lives, they team up with Alex—a player trapped in the game since 1996—to successfully return the jewel to a giant jaguar statue, yell the game's name, and break the curse.

Sweet Tooth

by Jeff Lemire · Adventure

The story is set a decade after a catastrophic pandemic known as "The Great Crumble" wiped out most of humanity, an event that mysteriously coincided with the emergence of hybrid babies born part-human, part-animal. Believing hybrids are hunted out of fear, Gus's father raises him in a secluded forest cabin, but after his father dies of the virus, a lonely Gus burns his fence down and is nearly captured by poachers called "Last Men". Rescued by a hardened, wandering loner named Tommy "Big Man" Jepperd, Gus convinces the reluctant traveler to protect him on an extraordinary cross-country expedition to Colorado to find his mother, Birdie. Along the way, they form a ragtag family of allies, escape the tyrannical General Abbot who hunts hybrids to engineer a selective cure, and ultimately journey all the way to Alaska where Gus finally learns that he was the very first hybrid ever created, eventually helping heal the rift between humanity and nature.
