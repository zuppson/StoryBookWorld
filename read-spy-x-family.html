Spy X Family

by Tatsuya Endo

1 Chapter · 1 min read

Synopsis

2022 anime based on the manga series by Tatsuya Endo. It is produced by Wit Studio and CloverWorks, and premiered in Japan on April 9, 2022.

Chapter 1: Operation Strix

To prevent a major conflict between the nations of Westalis and Ostania under Operation Strix, master spy Twilight adopts the persona of psychiatrist Loid Forger and builds a mock family to get close to the reclusive politician Donovan Desmond.

He adopts Anya, a young telepath who secretly knows his real identity, and marries Yor Briar, a quiet city hall worker who is actually a lethal hitman known as the Thorn Princess.

As Anya struggles to earn academic stars at the elite Eden Academy so Loid can approach his target, and as they later adopt a future-predicting dog named Bond, the trio must constantly hide their true professions from one another while slowly developing genuine, heartfelt affection as a real family.
