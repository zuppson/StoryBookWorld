Synopsis

(2010) is a fantasy adventure film about a teen who discovers he is a demigod son of Poseidon and must navigate a secret world of Greek mythology to prevent a war among the gods.

Chapter 1: A Demigod Called Percy

After being falsely accused by Zeus of stealing his master lightning bolt, sixteen-year-old Percy Jackson is taken to Camp Half-Blood—a secret training sanctuary for demigods—where he learns his true heritage as a son of Poseidon.

When Hades kidnaps Percy's mother to trade her for the missing bolt, Percy sneaks out of camp accompanied by his satyr protector Grover and Athena's warrior daughter Annabeth on a cross-country quest to rescue her from the Underworld. Along their journey, they battle mythological monsters like Medusa and the Hydra to collect magical pearls that will secure their escape.

Upon reaching the Underworld, Percy discovers that the rogue demigod Luke Castellan hid the bolt inside his shield to frame him, prompting Percy to defeat Luke in a fierce battle atop New York City, return the bolt to Zeus on Mount Olympus just in time, and successfully clear his father's name.
