John Wick

by Derek Kolstad · Action

A legendary and feared retired assassin who is deeply grieving the sudden death of his beloved wife, Helen. His fragile attempt at a peaceful life is shattered when arrogant Russian mob prince Iosef Tarasov and his thugs break into John's home, brutally assault him, steal his prized 1969 Mustang, and kill Daisy—a beagle puppy left to him as a final, posthumous gift from his wife to help him cope with his grief. Consumed by a desire for cold revenge, John unearths his meticulously concealed arsenal of weapons and re-enters the dark, highly structured criminal underworld governed by the rules of the Continental Hotel. Despite the desperate interventions of Iosef’s father, the powerful syndicate boss Viggo Tarasov—who fully understands John's terrifying reputation as the "Baba Yaga" and places a massive $2 million bounty on his head—John relentlessly dismantles Viggo's entire army. Surviving hits from rival assassins and receiving covert assistance from his old sniper mentor Marcus, John successfully hunts down and eliminates Iosef, completely decimates Viggo’s empire, and kills the mob boss in a brutal final confrontation, ultimately heading home with a newly adopted pitbull puppy to find closure.

Expendables

by Sylvester Stallone · Action

A group of elite mercenaries led by Barney Ross who travel to the fictional South American island nation of Vilena to overthrow a brutal dictator, General Garza. After starting with a hostage rescue mission in Somalia, Barney and his team—including Lee Christmas and Yin Yang—accept a contract from a mysterious operative named Mr. Church. During a scouting trip, Barney discovers that Garza is just a puppet controlled by a corrupt, former CIA agent named James Munroe, and after meeting Garza's defiant daughter Sandra, Barney decides to return and launch a full-scale rescue and assault on Munroe's compound.

The Roundup: Punishment

by Heo Myeong-haeng · Action

Teaming up with the Cyber Investigation Team, Ma discovers that the victim was coerced into working for a massive, multi-million dollar illegal online gambling syndicate. The ruthless ring is operated from Metro Manila by Baek Chang-ki (Kim Mu-yeol), a lethal, cold-blooded former special forces mercenary, who has partnered with tech-genius CEO Chang Dong-chul (Lee Dong-hwi) to establish a monopoly over the illegal casino market through kidnapping, assault, and murder. When a financial double-cross forces Baek to return to South Korea to violently settle a score, Detective Ma—alongside a hilariously tricked and conscripted returning gangster, Jang Yi-soo (Park Ji-hwan)—orchestrates a massive trap to dismantle the entire cybercrime syndicate and deliver Ma's signature powerhouse, bone-crushing justice.
