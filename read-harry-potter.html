Harry Potter

Synopsis

(2001–2011) is a fantasy film saga about an orphaned boy who discovers he is a wizard and leaves his cruel relatives to attend Hogwarts, where he learns magic and fights dark forces.

Chapter 1: The Boy With the Lightning Scar

Upon entering the magical world, Harry Potter learns that his parents were murdered by the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, who mysteriously vanished after failing to kill infant Harry, leaving the boy with a lightning-shaped scar. Alongside his best friends Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, Harry navigates the dangers of growing up at Hogwarts while Voldemort slowly rises back to power and gathers his loyal Death Eaters.

As the magical world plunges into a brutal wizarding war, Harry discovers that he is destiny's "Chosen One" and must hunt down Voldemort's Horcruxes—magical objects containing pieces of the Dark Lord's soul.

This quest ultimately culminates in an epic final battle at Hogwarts, where Harry sacrifices himself to destroy the final piece of Voldemort's soul within him, returns to life, and defeats the Dark Lord once and for all to restore peace.
