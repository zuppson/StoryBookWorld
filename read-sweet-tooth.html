Sweet Tooth

by Jeff Lemire

1 Chapter · 1 min read

Synopsis

(2021–2024) is a post-apocalyptic fantasy series about Gus, a half-human, half-deer hybrid boy, who leaves his isolated home to travel across a pandemic-ravaged America with a gruff protector to find his mother.

Chapter 1: The Boy Beyond the Fence

The story is set a decade after a catastrophic pandemic known as "The Great Crumble" wiped out most of humanity, an event that mysteriously coincided with the emergence of hybrid babies born part-human, part-animal.

Believing hybrids are hunted out of fear, Gus's father raises him in a secluded forest cabin, but after his father dies of the virus, a lonely Gus burns his fence down and is nearly captured by poachers called "Last Men".

Rescued by a hardened, wandering loner named Tommy "Big Man" Jepperd, Gus convinces the reluctant traveler to protect him on an extraordinary cross-country expedition to Colorado to find his mother, Birdie.

Along the way, they form a ragtag family of allies, escape the tyrannical General Abbot who hunts hybrids to engineer a selective cure, and ultimately journey all the way to Alaska where Gus finally learns that he was the very first hybrid ever created, eventually helping heal the rift between humanity and nature.
