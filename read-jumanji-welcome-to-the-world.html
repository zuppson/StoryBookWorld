Synopsis

(2017) is an adventure film where four high school teens get sucked into a magical video game, transforming into adult avatars who must survive a dangerous jungle to escape.

Chapter 1: Trapped Inside the Game

When high school students Spencer, Fridge, Bethany, and Martha find an old video game console while cleaning out their school's basement, starting the game accidentally transports them into the wild world of Jumanji as the wildly mismatched adult avatars they selected.

To escape and return to the real world, the teens must work together to complete a dangerous quest involving a stolen jewel, while navigating their avatars' specific video game strengths and hilarious weaknesses.

Facing aggressive wildlife, a ruthless warlord, and a limited pool of shared lives, they team up with Alex—a player trapped in the game since 1996—to successfully return the jewel to a giant jaguar statue, yell the game's name, and break the curse.
