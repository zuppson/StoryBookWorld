20th Century Girl

by Bang Woo-ri

1 Chapter · 1 min read

Synopsis

A nostalgic South Korean romance film set in 1999 that follows a bright 17-year-old high school student who closely monitors her best friend's crush, only to unexpectedly fall in love herself.

Chapter 1: A Promise to Spy

Follows 17-year-old Na Bo-ra, who promises to spy on a popular classmate named Baek Hyun-jin for her best friend, Yeon-du, who is traveling to the U.S. for critical heart surgery. While gathering information, Bo-ra inadvertently spends time with Hyun-jin's closest friend, Poong Woon-ho, and the two share a deeply intense, sweet, and mutual first love.

However, when a healthy Yeon-du returns, a devastating misunderstanding reveals that the boy Yeon-du actually liked all along was Woon-ho, prompting a heartbroken Bo-ra to sacrifice her own feelings to protect her best friend's happiness.

The relationship fractures completely when Woon-ho abruptly moves back to New Zealand, and decades later in 2019, an adult Bo-ra receives an art exhibition invitation and a videotape from Woon-ho's younger brother, uncovering the painful, long-hidden truth that Woon-ho never stopped loving her but tragically passed away years ago before they could ever reunite.
