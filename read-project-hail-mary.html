Project Hail Mary

by Andy Weir

1 Chapter · 1 min read

Synopsis

2026 SciFi movie with Ryan Gosling stars as an amnesiac teacher on a lonely space mission to save Earth from a sun-eating microbe, teaming up with an intelligent spider-like alien to stop total extinction.

Chapter 1: The Man Who Woke Up in Space

A middle-school science teacher and former molecular biologist named Ryland Grace is recruited by Eva Stratt to investigate a mysterious space microbe called Astrophase that is draining energy from the sun and causing global cooling. After discovering that the nearby star Tau Ceti is mysteriously immune to the parasite, Grace joins a desperate one-way interstellar mission aboard the Hail Mary spacecraft.

After waking up from a long-duration coma with total amnesia next to his two deceased crewmates, he pieces together his past, encounters an alien engineer he nicknames Rocky whose star is also dying, and works alongside him to develop a cure.

After solving the crisis, Grace realizes Rocky's ship has lost its power source, prompting him to launch his data probes back to Earth and burn his remaining fuel to save his alien friend.
