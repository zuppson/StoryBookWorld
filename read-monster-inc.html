Monster Inc.

by Pete Docter

1 Chapter · 1 min read

Synopsis

(2001) is a heartwarming animated comedy set in the city of Monstropolis, where the local power company generates electricity by harvesting the screams of human children.

Chapter 1: A Little Girl in Monstropolis

Sulley and Mike as they desperately try to smuggle the little human girl, whom they nickname "Boo," safely back to her bedroom before the authorities or the Child Detection Agency (CDA) discover her. Along the way, the duo realizes that human children are not toxic or dangerous as monster society has always believed.

However, their mission becomes a dangerous rescue operation when they uncover a sinister corporate conspiracy orchestrated by Sulley's chameleon-like rival, Randall Boggs, and their own beloved CEO, Henry J. Waternoose, who have built a machine to forcefully extract screams from kidnapped children.

After being banished to the human world and fighting their way back through a massive, motorized closet door warehouse, Sulley and Mike successfully banish Randall, expose Waternoose's crimes to the CDA, and return Boo home.

With the factory facing an energy crisis, Sulley revolutionizes the company by proving that children's laughter is ten times more powerful than screams, saving the city and allowing him to ultimately reunite with Boo.
