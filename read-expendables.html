(2010) is an action movie about a tight-knit group of elite mercenaries.

Chapter 1: The Mission to Vilena

A group of elite mercenaries led by Barney Ross who travel to the fictional South American island nation of Vilena to overthrow a brutal dictator, General Garza. After starting with a hostage rescue mission in Somalia, Barney and his team—including Lee Christmas and Yin Yang—accept a contract from a mysterious operative named Mr. Church.

During a scouting trip, Barney discovers that Garza is just a puppet controlled by a corrupt, former CIA agent named James Munroe, and after meeting Garza's defiant daughter Sandra, Barney decides to return and launch a full-scale rescue and assault on Munroe's compound.
