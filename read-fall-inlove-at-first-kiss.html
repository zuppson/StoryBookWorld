Fall Inlove At First Kiss

by Frankie Chen

1 Chapter · 1 min read

Synopsis

2019 Chinese-Taiwanese romantic comedy film directed by Frankie Chen. The movie is a popular adaptation of the classic Japanese manga series Itazura na Kiss by Kaoru Tada.

Chapter 1: An Accidental Kiss

The plot centers on Yuan Xiangqin (played by Lin Yun), an ordinary and academically struggling student, who falls instantly in love with Jiang Zhishu (played by Darren Wang), an icy genius with an IQ of 200. Their paths collide during a literal stumble on their first day of school, resulting in an accidental kiss that sparks Xiangqin's unrequited devotion.

After a series of rejections and an earthquake that leaves her family homeless, Xiangqin unexpectedly moves into the home of Zhishu's parents, forcing the polar opposites to live under the same roof.
