John Wick

Synopsis

(2024) A retired fighter comes back to seek justice after his beloved pet is harmed and his car is stolen.

Chapter 1: The Quiet Life Ends

A legendary and feared retired assassin who is deeply grieving the sudden death of his beloved wife, Helen. His fragile attempt at a peaceful life is shattered when arrogant Russian mob prince Iosef Tarasov and his thugs break into John's home, brutally assault him, steal his prized 1969 Mustang, and kill Daisy—a beagle puppy left to him as a final, posthumous gift from his wife to help him cope with his grief.

Consumed by a desire for cold revenge, John unearths his meticulously concealed arsenal of weapons and re-enters the dark, highly structured criminal underworld governed by the rules of the Continental Hotel.

Despite the desperate interventions of Iosef’s father, the powerful syndicate boss Viggo Tarasov—who fully understands John's terrifying reputation as the "Baba Yaga" and places a massive $2 million bounty on his head—John relentlessly dismantles Viggo's entire army.

Surviving hits from rival assassins and receiving covert assistance from his old sniper mentor Marcus, John successfully hunts down and eliminates Iosef, completely decimates Viggo’s empire, and kills the mob boss in a brutal final confrontation, ultimately heading home with a newly adopted pitbull puppy to find closure.
