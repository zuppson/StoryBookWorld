Synopsis

A clumsy British art gallery guard is sent to Los Angeles under the guise of an art scholar to oversee a famous painting's unveiling, causing massive chaos.

Chapter 1: The Masterpiece Mishap

follows an inept British museum guard who is sent to Los Angeles under the guise of an elite art scholar to oversee the unveiling of the $50 million masterpiece Whistler's Mother.

After accidentally ruining the priceless painting with ink and paint thinner, he sneaks into the gallery at night and replaces it with a poster replica coated in egg whites, successfully fooling the public before returning to London with the defaced original canvas.
