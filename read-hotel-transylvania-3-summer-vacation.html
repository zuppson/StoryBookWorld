Hotel Transylvania 3: Summer Vacation

by Genndy Tartakovsky

1 Chapter · 1 min read

Synopsis

(2018) is a vibrant and zany animated comedy that follows Count Dracula and his monster pack as they embark on a luxury family cruise organized by his daughter, Mavis, to give him a break from managing the hotel.

Chapter 1: All Aboard the Monster Cruise

The monster family as they sail through the Bermuda Triangle toward the lost city of Atlantis, unaware that Ericka and her fully mechanized great-grandfather have plotted a trap. Despite Ericka making multiple undercover attempts to assassinate Dracula during the voyage, his genuine kindness and affection slowly win her over, causing her to question her family's ancestral hatred.

The conflict reaches its climax in Atlantis, where Van Helsing uses an ancient Instrument of Destruction to play an evil melody that brainwashes a giant kraken into attacking the monsters. In a goofy, high-stakes climax, Dracula's son-in-law Johnny counters the curse by staging a DJ battle and playing the upbeat song "Macarena," which frees the kraken and restores peace.

Ultimately, Ericka rejects her legacy, saves Dracula's life, and confesses her love for him, prompting the reformed Van Helsing family to accept the monsters and culminating in Dracula successfully proposing to Ericka back home.
