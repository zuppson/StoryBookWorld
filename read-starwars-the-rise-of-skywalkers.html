Starwars: The Rise of Skywalkers

by J.J. Abrams

1 Chapter · 1 min read

Synopsis

(2019) Follows the Resistance as they face the return of Emperor Palpatine. Meanwhile, Rey completes her Jedi training while navigating her mysterious past and her intense, Force-connected bond with Kylo Ren.

Chapter 1: The Secret of Exegol

Follows Rey, Finn, and Poe Dameron as they race against time to locate Exegol, a hidden Sith world where a resurrected Emperor Palpatine has secretly constructed a massive armada called the Final Order. Kylo Ren hunts Rey down to bring her to Palpatine, only to discover that Rey is actually the Emperor's granddaughter.

After a series of fierce confrontations—including a climactic duel on the ruins of the Death Star where Leia Organa passes away to redeem her son—Kylo abandons the dark side and reclaims his identity as Ben Solo. Rey travels to Exegol to confront Palpatine, who attempts to absorb her and Ben's unique Force dyad power to restore his youth.

Supported by the spirits of past Jedi and a massive civilian fleet assembled by Lando Calrissian to crush the Sith armada, Rey channels the power of all Jedi to permanently destroy Palpatine at the cost of her life.

Ben uses his remaining life force to revive her, dying in the process, which allows Rey to return to Tatooine, bury the lightsabers of Luke and Leia, and honor their legacy by adopting the name Rey Skywalker.
