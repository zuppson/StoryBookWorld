Chapter 1: The One Wish Willow

A socially awkward store clerk named Bear uses a supernatural artifact called the "One Wish Willow" to force his unrequited crush, Nikki, to love him unconditionally, unintentionally trapping her true soul in purgatory while manifesting a violently possessive, corrupted version of her in the real world.

As this artificial Nikki's obsession spirals into extreme manipulation and graphic violence against anyone close to them, Bear desperately tries to break the spell, only to find the cosmic contract is completely irreversible.

The nightmare reaches a tragic climax when the real Nikki briefly claws her way to consciousness and uses a second willow to wish that Bear loves her until death; this triggers Bear's sudden demise in her arms, which finally breaks the curse and forces Nikki to wake up permanently to face the horrifying trauma and wreckage left behind.
