Chapter 1: A Detour Into the Woods

Follows college student Chris Flynn, who takes a detour through the backwoods of West Virginia to avoid a traffic jam, only to accidentally crash his car into a vehicle belonging to five stranded hikers. Left without cellular service, the group splits up to look for a phone and discovers an isolated mountain cabin filled with gruesome human remains.

They quickly realize they are being hunted by Three Finger, Saw Tooth, and One Eye—a trio of deformed, cannibalistic inbred mountain men who use cruel hunting traps to slaughter travelers for meat.

After a desperate and bloody game of cat-and-mouse through the forest, watchtowers, and rivers, most of the group is brutally murdered, leaving only Chris and a hiker named Jessie Burlingame to successfully fight back, kill the cannibals, and escape alive in the killers' own truck.
