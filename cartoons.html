Toy Story 5

by Andrew Stanton · Cartoons

The film shifts the spotlight to Jessie, who takes charge as the leader to help an isolated, screen-obsessed Bonnie break away from her tablet and make real-life friends. When Jessie and Bullseye get separated from Bonnie during a dance-class sleepover, they are taken to the old house of Jessie's original owner, Emily. There, Jessie deals with old feelings of abandonment, only to discover that Emily loved her deeply and even named her daughter, Blae, after her. Meanwhile, Woody returns from his travels with Bo Peep to help the group, and Buzz Lightyear gets caught up in a hilarious subplot involving fifty upgraded, stranded clone models of himself trying to reach Star Command. Realizing that a guilty Lilypad has thrown herself into a donation box after accidentally cyberbullying Bonnie, the toys put aside their rivalry and work together on a daring rescue mission. By utilizing both traditional playtime imagination and the high-tech capabilities of the clone Buzz army, the toys safely reunite and orchestrate a meeting between Bonnie and Blae, successfully teaching Bonnie the value of real friendship and culminating in a joyful, toy-orchestrated wedding for Jessie and Buzz.

Monster Inc.

by Pete Docter · Cartoons

Sulley and Mike as they desperately try to smuggle the little human girl, whom they nickname "Boo," safely back to her bedroom before the authorities or the Child Detection Agency (CDA) discover her. Along the way, the duo realizes that human children are not toxic or dangerous as monster society has always believed. However, their mission becomes a dangerous rescue operation when they uncover a sinister corporate conspiracy orchestrated by Sulley's chameleon-like rival, Randall Boggs, and their own beloved CEO, Henry J. Waternoose, who have built a machine to forcefully extract screams from kidnapped children. After being banished to the human world and fighting their way back through a massive, motorized closet door warehouse, Sulley and Mike successfully banish Randall, expose Waternoose's crimes to the CDA, and return Boo home. With the factory facing an energy crisis, Sulley revolutionizes the company by proving that children's laughter is ten times more powerful than screams, saving the city and allowing him to ultimately reunite with Boo.

Hotel Transylvania 3: Summer Vacation

by Genndy Tartakovsky · Cartoons

The monster family as they sail through the Bermuda Triangle toward the lost city of Atlantis, unaware that Ericka and her fully mechanized great-grandfather have plotted a trap. Despite Ericka making multiple undercover attempts to assassinate Dracula during the voyage, his genuine kindness and affection slowly win her over, causing her to question her family's ancestral hatred. The conflict reaches its climax in Atlantis, where Van Helsing uses an ancient Instrument of Destruction to play an evil melody that brainwashes a giant kraken into attacking the monsters. In a goofy, high-stakes climax, Dracula's son-in-law Johnny counters the curse by staging a DJ battle and playing the upbeat song "Macarena," which frees the kraken and restores peace. Ultimately, Ericka rejects her legacy, saves Dracula's life, and confesses her love for him, prompting the reformed Van Helsing family to accept the monsters and culminating in Dracula successfully proposing to Ericka back home.
