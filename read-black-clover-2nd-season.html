Synopsis

(2026) is the highly anticipated seasonal continuation of the hit dark fantasy anime, returning after a five-year hiatus to adapt the definitive conclusion of Yūki Tabata's manga series.

Chapter 1: The Raid on the Spade Kingdom

Picking up exactly where the original 170-episode run ended, the story plunges into the high-stakes Spade Kingdom Raid Arc as the Magic Knights launch a desperate, coordinated assault against the tyrannical Dark Triad to rescue the abducted Captains Yami Sukehiro and William Vengeance.

With the villains attempting to open the underworld gates via the catastrophic Tree of Qliphoth ritual, Asta must push past his physical limits to master his powerful "Devil Union" form alongside his anti-magic devil, Liebe.

Following the grueling fallout of the devil convergence, the conflict rapidly escalates into a final, world-ending sprint against Lucius Zogratis—the most formidable threat the Magic Knights have ever faced—propelling Asta and Yuno into an ultimate showdown to protect humanity and fulfill their shared dream of becoming the Wizard King.
